Beyyond Cares.

Access to good technology shouldn't stop at businesses that can afford it. Beyyond Tech is committed to supporting local NGOs and NPOs — the organisations doing essential work on tight budgets.

We give back in three ways: our time, direct contributions, and the same IT expertise we sell to paying clients — offered to qualifying non-profits at reduced or no cost.

If you run or know a registered NGO or NPO that could use help with security, a website, or day-to-day IT, we'd like to hear from you.
